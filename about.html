About POPSY ADONIS TV

Your best assured cure to boredom—bringing EKSU entertainment, campus connections, and business promotions together.

POPSY ADONIS

WhatsApp Television

Entertainment built around EKSU

Popsy Adonis TV is a student lifestyle and entertainment hub serving the Ekiti State University community. It brings campus nightlife, party information and ticket enquiries, matchmaking connects, viral content, music, and promotional opportunities into one easy-to-reach WhatsApp platform.

Our aim is to make it easier for students to stay informed, find entertainment, and discover local businesses and events, while giving brands a direct way to reach an engaged campus audience.
